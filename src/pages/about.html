About us

Document tools that respect your privacy

We think converting a PDF shouldn't mean handing your documents to a stranger's server. So we built YoPdfs to do the work right on your device.

Why we built YoPdfs

Students upload assignments, job seekers upload résumés and ID photos, and businesses upload contracts and invoices to online PDF tools every day. Most of those services copy every file to their servers. We wanted something faster and safer.

YoPdfs runs modern, open-source engines inside your browser — the same way a web game or video editor runs locally. Your file is read, processed and handed back to you without ever being uploaded.

What powers it

  • pdf-lib and PDF.js for creating, editing and rendering PDFs.
  • qpdf (compiled to WebAssembly) for AES-256 encryption and unlocking.
  • Tesseract for OCR in 20+ languages.
  • An on-device AI segmentation model for background removal.
  • SheetJS, jsPDF and docx-preview for Office conversions.

Who it's for

College students handling assignments and applications, professionals and job seekers polishing documents, and businesses that need dependable tools without sending sensitive files to third parties.

Our promises

  • Core tools stay free, with no watermarks.
  • Your files are processed on your device.
  • No account required — ever — to use a tool.
  • Clear, honest pricing for any future premium features.
