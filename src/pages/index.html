FAQ

Good questions

Is YoPdfs really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no watermark and no sign-up. Optional accounts let you keep a history of the tools you use, and future Pro plans will add extras for heavy users — the core tools stay free.

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No. YoPdfs processes files inside your browser using open-source engines (pdf-lib, PDF.js, qpdf, Tesseract and an on-device AI model). Your files never reach our servers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — YoPdfs is fully responsive and works in modern mobile browsers on Android and iPhone. Very large files are faster on a computer.

Why should I create an account?

An account is optional. It saves your tool history and preferences across visits, and it will unlock Pro features when they launch.

Is there a file size limit?

There's no server limit because nothing is uploaded. Practical limits depend on your device's memory — files of a few hundred MB work well on most computers.
