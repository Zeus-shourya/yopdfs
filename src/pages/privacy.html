Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: your files are processed in your browser and are never uploaded to YoPdfs. If you create an account, we store your profile details and a history of which tools you used (not your files). We don't sell your data.

1. Your files

All PDF and image tools run locally in your web browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Files you select are read into your device's memory, processed there, and offered back to you as a download. YoPdfs servers never receive, store or see your files or their contents. Closing the tab clears them from memory.

Some tools download processing engines the first time you use them — for example the OCR language data (from jsDelivr) and the background-removal AI model (from IMG.LY's content delivery network). These are downloads to your device; your files are not sent to those services.

2. Information we collect

If you create an account

  • Name, email address, role (student / professional / business) and, if you choose, your college or company and phone number.
  • Your password, which is hashed in your browser before it is sent and hashed again with a unique salt before storage. We never store or see your plain password.
  • Sign-up date, login times and the type of device used (mobile, tablet or desktop).
  • Whether you agreed to receive product updates.

When you use a tool (with or without an account)

  • The tool used, number of files, total input and output size, processing time, whether it succeeded, device type, and a random anonymous visitor ID stored in your browser.
  • We never record file names or file contents.

When you contact us or join a list

  • The name, email and message you send through the contact form, newsletter or waitlist.

3. How we use information

  • To provide your account and show your tool history.
  • To understand which tools are used so we can improve them.
  • To reply to your messages and, if you opted in, send occasional product updates.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

4. Where data is stored

Account, usage and contact records are stored in a private spreadsheet controlled by YoPdfs (hosted on Google Sheets or on our own server). Access is limited to the YoPdfs team. We keep account data while your account is active and usage records for up to 24 months.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (light or dark), your login session and an anonymous visitor ID. If analytics or advertising are enabled on the site, Google Analytics or Google AdSense may set their own cookies; where required we ask for consent first.

6. Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run YoPdfs (such as spreadsheet hosting) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

7. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile on the My account page. To export or delete your account data, contact us through the contact page using “Privacy request” — we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR) you may have additional rights.

8. Children

YoPdfs is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.

10. Contact

Questions? Reach us at hello@yopdfs.com or via the contact page.
